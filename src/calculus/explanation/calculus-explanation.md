In January 2025, I had just finished a contract at LinkedIn Learning and wanted to make my own [educational website](/calculus){new}. But I had never made a website before, and I made every programming-based choice incorrectly.

A lot of time was wasted going down rabbit holes I should have never been in, but that wasted time gave me knowledge and opinions on not just web development, but design, education, and programming as a whole.

I have left the website untouched, except for the minimal changes required for the paths to work. I am endlessly grateful that I took on this project, and it is perhaps the first project I can look at without suffering, even with its many warts.
